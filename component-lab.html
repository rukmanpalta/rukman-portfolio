Accordion

Accessible expand/collapse content

A React or AEM accordion can track open/closed state and render each item from reusable content data.

Use a real button, update aria-expanded, connect controls with aria-controls, and keep keyboard behavior natural for WCAG-compliant sections.

Accordions are common in FAQ, patient journey, and content- heavy enterprise pages that need clear information hierarchy.
