Accordion

Accessible expand/collapse content

A React Accordion can track open/closed state with useState and render each item from an array.

Use a real button, update aria-expanded, connect controls with aria-controls, and keep keyboard behavior natural.

Accordions are common in FAQ, product detail, support, and content-heavy enterprise pages.
