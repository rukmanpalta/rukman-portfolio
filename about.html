About

Frontend developer focused on responsive, accessible, production-ready web experiences.

I bring a blend of frontend development, AEM implementation, content workflows, QA collaboration, and release support. My strongest work is turning designs and requirements into polished, mobile-first pages that are maintainable and easy for teams to use.

Professional summary

Frontend developer with over five years of experience building responsive, WCAG-compliant AEM and React applications for enterprise healthcare and media brands. My resume includes work for Eversana, GDH, Real World, and Xapads, delivering patient, HCP, and publish-ready page experiences.

I collaborated with UX, QA, creative, product, delivery, and development teams in Agile sprint cycles. I also supported requirement clarification, blocker tracking, QA/UAT, and release readiness so work could move smoothly from design to production.
