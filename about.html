About

Frontend developer focused on responsive, accessible, production-ready web experiences.

I bring a blend of frontend development, AEM implementation, content workflows, QA collaboration, and release support. My strongest work is turning designs and requirements into polished, mobile-first pages that are maintainable and accessible.

Professional summary

Frontend Developer with 6+ years of experience creating responsive, user-friendly web experiences across healthcare, pharmaceutical, and digital advertising industries. My background is centered around Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), HTML, CSS/SCSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript, with hands-on experience supporting enterprise websites for global pharmaceutical brands.

I enjoy turning designs into polished digital experiences, solving complex frontend challenges, and working closely with designers, QA teams, product managers, and developers to deliver high-quality solutions. Throughout my career, I've focused on accessibility, performance, reusable component development, and building experiences that are both intuitive for users and easy for teams to maintain.
